Adds an **Invoice Name** to product templates and prints it on customer
invoice lines in place of the product name.

The name a product goes by internally is not always the one a customer
should read on an invoice: it may carry an internal code, a variant
detail, or simply be longer than the invoice layout allows. This module
keeps the product name as it is and gives the invoice its own wording.

Usage
=====

Set **Invoice Name** on the *Sales* tab of the product. Customer invoice
and credit note lines for that product are then labelled with it; the
*Quotation Description*, when set, still follows on the line below, as
it does for the product name. A product with no invoice name is labelled
as before.

The label is replaced only while it is the one the product would give. A
label typed by hand on a line is kept, and so is a label carried over
from a sales order line, since the order line is what the invoice line
is created from. Vendor bills are not affected.
