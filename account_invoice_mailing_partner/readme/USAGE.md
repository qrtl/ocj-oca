When an invoice is created, **Invoice Mailing Address** (under the *Other Info*
tab) is filled in from the partner. It can be changed on the invoice as long as
it is not posted, for instance to send a single invoice to another department.

The mailing address is not propagated back from the partner to the invoices
already created: changing the default on a contact only affects new documents.

The recipient address block of the invoice report shows the mailing address when
one is set, and the billing partner otherwise. The customer tax ID is always
printed for the billing partner.
