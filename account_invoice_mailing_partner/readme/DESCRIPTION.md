In some businesses the printed invoice has to be sent to a contact that is not
the billing partner: a head office that collects the invoices of its branches,
an outsourced accounting firm, or a specific department of the customer.

This module separates the *billing partner* from the *invoice mailing address*.
The invoice is still issued to, and accounted for, the billing partner; only the
recipient address printed on the document is replaced.

It adds an **Invoice Mailing Address** field to:

- contacts, where it acts as the default for the documents of that customer;
- customer invoices, where the default can be overridden per document.
